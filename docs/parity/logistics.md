# Logistics (LOG)

Scope: unit reserves, shortages and withdrawal, territory supply, supply stores, trucks and convoys, allied relief, point supply lines, upkeep, and the logistics parts of the client. All paths are relative to `reference/ww2-rts` at e4e579a. "Live" means a server room; "headless" means `createGame` called directly (tests, tools).

The standalone reserve helpers are ported in `rust/crates/core/src/logistics.rs`, with original-JavaScript fixtures for 60 enablement contexts, 118 definitions, 708 initializations and 618 state/stock/query steps. This covers the pure reserve portions of LOG-01 through LOG-10 and LOG-15. LOG-03/04/10/15 are ported from the complete selected reserve checks below. The remaining listed rows retain their separate initialization, movement/combat, withdrawal and convoy requirements.

The standalone convoy component is now ported in `supply_convoys.rs`: startup stores, stock/grants, support spending, local and territorial service, fleet/jobs, route refresh and repair, delivery/return, manual/resume/death and recipient projections. Its original-function oracle covers 12 traces with 540 steps, six payer-pool cases and five support cases. Route/handoff/territorial-rate hooks are explicit caller inputs. Supply-network floods, withdrawal, match integration, World region-rate projection and client/wire delivery remain pending. LOG-29 is ported by the isolated store-restock evidence below; this does not complete the other LOG rows.

The bounded ordinary-Conquest `Match::advance_supply_withdrawal` phase now passes its original nine-trace, 103-transition replay and free-truck cleanup test. It uses canonical reserves, controls, paid field cancellation and actual recipient routes/handoffs. The checked successor passes 28 affected debug checks and all 215 locked release workspace tests, with strict Clippy, formatting and release FFI. Full unit-loop integration, other modes and client delivery remain pending; LOG-12 through LOG-14 are not marked complete by this phase alone.
