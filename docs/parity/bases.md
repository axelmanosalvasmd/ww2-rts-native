# Bases, economy and progression (BASE)

Source: `reference/ww2-rts` at e4e579a. Evidence paths are relative to that folder. Units: MP, seconds, metres; one cell is 2 m. "Construction modes" means Classic and World Conquest (`isConstructionMode`, `shared/sim.js:384`). "Skirmish modes" means Conquest, Assault, Annihilation and Horde (`isSkirmishBaseMode`, `shared/sim.js:385`). JS tests are cited as file plus check name or assertion message.

The wallet and population foundation is implemented as a component, with 37 original-JavaScript traces and 217 income steps. It does not run matches, initialize mode wallets, capture territory, buy units or advance production. Production queues and research now have a separate canonical component. Unit creation, building lifecycle, persistence and public snapshots remain outside that component. The rows below remain in progress until their full checks pass.

The ordinary construction continuation is source-only. It has typed paid Build/Assist over the existing queue, paired numeric builder identity, staged placement/routing and source-positioned ordered crews for eight static ground kinds. Its unmodified source recorder covers 29 cases, 113 actions, 1,919 full steps and 2,013 snapshots, including ordered repair budgets. The real advance_tick consumer requires the sole caller's phase wiring; native and final independent provider checks are pending. Depot/coastal/cache and cancellation lifecycles, Classic/World admission and client delivery remain open. Construction rows stay in progress until their complete matching checks pass.
