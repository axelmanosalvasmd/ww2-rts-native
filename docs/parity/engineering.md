# Engineering, destruction and environment (ENG)

Source: `reference/ww2-rts` at e4e579a. Evidence paths are relative to that folder. Units: MP, seconds, metres; one cell is 2 m; levels are 2.5 m. Cell letters: T trench, # sandbags, X wire, Y tank traps, N mine overlay, = bridge, A Field Hospital, + crater, R rubble, Q vehicle wreck, K building footprint, B house, H hedge, O woods, M mud, F ford, W river, D road. JS tests are cited as file plus check name or assertion message.

Conquest recovery/repair work is source-written in `match_recovery.rs`, including canonical section restoration, immediate medic routing and team mine detection. The recorder reproduces 23 traces and 133 actions from the original helpers and captured late phases. Native differential verification and incoming provision-hook integration remain pending. This does not implement paid field construction, general building crews or bridge placement.

## Field fortifications (FORTS)

Native ordinary field-work progress: `field_works.rs` has paid admission, sight-masked placement and timed cell execution for trench, sandbags, wire, traps, Gun Pit, minefield, known-mine clearing and Field Hospital. Its detailed Dig record is canonical; cancellation removes it synchronously. The original-source recorder covers 22 traces and 367 actions. Actual command/tick dispatch, Dig path eligibility, garrison exit and story application are pending. Queues, shared projects, bridge/concrete placement and fill work are outside this slice. Affected rows remain in progress.

Mutable-world progress (issue #62): the canonical World layer now implements terrain mutation, packed cell state, ordered journaling, cleanup and base-building section registration. Original-source traces include overlapping sections, retaining displaced section values in historical storage. Damage, repair, collapse and falling simulation remain unimplemented by this slice; affected rows stay in progress.

## Existing limitations

- A fortification cell whose ground changed before its turn is skipped without refund; a concrete cell blocked 20 times is dropped without refund (`shared/sim.js:5397-5405`).
- Shoals are worked out once per match; a bridge built later does not move destroyers (`shared/sim.js:2666`).
- Wrecks are capped at 40 and the floor is level -2 (`shared/sim.js:157-158`).
- Start depths from `cellNoise` are not symmetric, so mirrored maps can favour a spawn (DESIGN.md:2028-2030).
- The weather changes at most once per match (`shared/weather.js:60-71`).
- Debris never damages or pushes units; it only becomes rubble or a wreck obstacle (`shared/debris-motion.js:1`; test-engine-debris.js 'falling section contacts do not crush living units').
- A house cell whose section fails opens to ground; it blocks only where settled rubble lands (`shared/sim.js:2577`, `:5158-5161`).
