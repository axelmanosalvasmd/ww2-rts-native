## Ordinary numeric-sort consumers

Ordinary Classic resource setup preserves the prototype's first-discovery pair order with an insertion-ordered vector and explicit deduplication. A sorted set imposed a different order on the resulting candidate nodes. Remembered Classic buildings preserve discovery order, with identity validation checking uniqueness rather than requiring ID order. HQ fortification sorts its reused crew array before skipping a completed or tier-locked HQ, preserving that ordering for the next HQ's tied distances.

The ordinary Classic construction command borrows the canonical elimination node list for placement. Successful publication claims the selected node; cancellation, retirement and elimination release that claim. The AI receives public node positions and richness and derives claims from allied delivered depots and remembered enemy depots. Engineer reservations are private serializable policy state. No hidden live depot coordinates enter the policy.

New AI facts extend only the internal detached observation. Aircraft readiness comes from the owner's canonical Base state. Canonical Base and Rearm aircraft may sit outside the map, as in prototype `shared/sim.js:2178-2189`; the runtime AI projection permits finite aircraft poses there while ground positions, paths and quiet observations retain their existing bounds. Mine ownership, detected mines and damaged terrain come from remembered recipient rows; authored bridge cells and terrain baselines come from the public map. Sorting retains the existing numeric-difference comparator and its stable zero and NaN behavior. World room admission and all nine World sort expressions remain deferred.
