its team building memory. Admission requires no authored groups and only Say,
objective activation and objective completion actions. The same scenario
validator, schedule, checkpoint codec and recipient policy serve both ordinary
Conquest and this Classic subset. Classic spawn and damage actuators remain
refused by both startup and the action preflight. World rooms stay closed.

This removes an internal constructor limitation without adding wire fields.
The output retains `shared/sim.js:6036-6037` ghost rows and
`shared/scenarios.js:159-175` mission rows at prototype `e4e579a`. Normal Classic
elimination and ending remain authoritative. The composed lifecycle test checks
