recorder deliberately installs reserve rows while logistics is disabled to
isolate repair-budget arithmetic and missing-first-worker behavior. Those rows
are valid inputs to the construction component, but are not an admissible
hosted match after the logistics integration. The witness now separates the
two component turns from 1,917 complete runner turns and checks atomic refusal
at the full boundary. World admission is unchanged.

Insertion-ordered JSON is required by authored localization. It must not change
public map-array order or the independent fixture loader's canonical terrain
health order. Sort those explicit collections, not localized author data.

The reconciled save-chain contract remains authoritative: legacy authored_mission
owners are decode-only and explicitly refused, including version-1 room saves.
