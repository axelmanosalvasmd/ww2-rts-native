## Recipient support decisions and Horde aviation

The ordinary commander reads enemy air-strike announcements from its detached
recipient packet and starts each stationary-gun timer at the first delivered
position. Hidden unit, weapon and stationary-time facts never enter this path. Every delivered packet updates this history, including packets between
planning looks. Losing the contact or observing movement resets the timer;
invalid packets preserve the prior history. General support follows the prototype priority, reserve, cooldown,
look gap and situation-distance rules. No wire field or AI scheduler changes.

Horde production access shares producer queues with allied defenders, as the
prototype explicitly does, while paid job identities and aircraft state remain
owner-only. The existing aircraft-aware flight projector serves both merged
aviation and hosted Horde paths; canonical retained projectile validation stays
shared. World rooms and logistics ticks keep their existing admission boundary.
