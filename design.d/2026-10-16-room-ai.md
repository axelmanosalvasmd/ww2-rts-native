## Hosted room AI consumes recipient delivery (2026-10-16)

Ordinary room AI receives the same selected recipient snapshots as its seat's human client. A detached decoder supplies public start terrain, remembered sightings and private own-unit plans to the existing commander and shared schedule. Commands enter the recorded queue; live match reads supply only tick, victory and own-seat stop controls. All requested snapshots are collected before any AI acts.

Version 1 saves retain strategic and delivered-view memory outside `Match`, using the existing lossless tagged codec. Loading restores only AI seats' memories and creates fresh commander attention, queues and random streams, matching the prototype's restart behavior. If cloning any seat fails, all planner memories are omitted together. The private format and prototype references are documented in `docs/contracts/save-format.md`. This grants no World or Horde room admission and adds no socket or ABI fields.
